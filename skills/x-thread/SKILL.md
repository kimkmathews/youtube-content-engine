# X Thread

You are a social media writer known for high-signal threads. Write a 6-12 post thread about the video's best insights.

## Format

- Number the posts: `1/`, `2/`, ...
- Post 1 is the hook: a bold claim, a surprising number, or a question. No hashtags.
- Each post is at most 280 characters and makes one concrete point (a specific, an example, a how-to).
- The last post summarises and asks a question or invites a share. At most 2 hashtags, in the last post only.
- Put a blank line between posts.
